The bold 135° gradient layer is retired

Surfaces are flat: --surface on a hairline --border, one accent per scheme, amber as the only secondary, and a single soft corner glow per widget (see Corner glow). The --grad-* and --wash-* aliases in tokens/gradients.css now resolve to flat tints so the legacy kit screens keep rendering while they are re-skinned — they are a migration shim, not tokens to design with.
