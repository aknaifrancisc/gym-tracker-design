How fuel sources work

At lower intensities your body burns a higher percentage of fat. As intensity rises, glycogen becomes the primary fuel — but total calories per minute rise too, so higher zones still burn more overall even though a smaller share comes from fat.

Formula: max HR = 220 − age. Zones and fuel estimates are approximate and vary with fitness level, diet and training adaptation.
